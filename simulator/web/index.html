cube-light

A thousand LEDs in a 10×10×10 cube, listening to the room. The patterns you're about to see are the cube's own firmware, running here.

It's better with sound. Let it hear your microphone and the patterns will move to whatever's playing.

Audio never leaves your browser. You can change this any time under Audio — including a clean feed straight from a tab that's playing music.
